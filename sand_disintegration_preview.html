How This Sand & Air Disintegration Works in Production

1 Bounding Rect Capture

When the admin clicks Delete, we capture the exact pixel bounds `getBoundingClientRect()` of the table row and sample its natural palette (text, badges, card surface).

2 60 FPS Particle Physics

1,400 micro sand grains ignite across the card. Each grain experiences atmospheric air drag, curl-noise breeze turbulence, and exponential opacity decay.

3 Zero-Jank Layout Glide

While the sand cloud drifts into the sky, the remaining table rows beneath gently slide upwards into place without abrupt layout flashing.
